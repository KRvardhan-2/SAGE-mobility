# SAGE — Location-Aware Speed Safety

**SAGE** is a road-safety platform developed by **Offguard** to make vehicle speed more responsive to the surrounding environment.

SAGE uses geographic safety zones to identify locations where controlled speed is important and enables context-aware speed safety.

## The Problem

Road conditions and safety requirements change from one location to another.

A vehicle may move from an ordinary road into an area such as a school, hospital, educational institution, or other sensitive zone where lower speeds are more appropriate.

Traditional road-safety approaches often depend on signs, driver awareness, and human reaction. SAGE explores a preventive approach where the vehicle can respond to its geographic context.

## The SAGE Approach

SAGE creates a digital safety layer between geographic location and vehicle speed behaviour.

The core concept is:

**Location → Safety Zone → Context Recognition → Speed-Safety Response**

This allows speed behaviour to be associated with specific geographic areas rather than treating every road segment in the same way.

## Key Features

- Location-aware speed safety
- Geographic safety zones
- Zone-specific speed rules
- Offline-capable geographic data
- Updateable safety-zone information
- Designed for vehicle and mobility-system integration
- Scalable from controlled pilots to larger deployments

## Why SAGE

SAGE is designed around a simple principle:

> **Speed should adapt to the road around us.**

Rather than relying only on warnings after a risk has developed, SAGE aims to support preventive safety by recognising locations where controlled speed behaviour is important.

The long-term goal of SAGE is to contribute to safer mobility by:

- Encouraging appropriate speeds in sensitive areas

- Making vehicle behaviour more context-aware

- Enabling organisations to implement location-based safety policies
