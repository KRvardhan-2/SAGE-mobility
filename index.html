SAGE is a location-aware speed safety platform designed to identify designated safety zones and enable context-aware speed control. The goal is simple: help prevent excessive speed where people are most vulnerable.

01 — THE PROBLEM

Roads change. Speed rules and risks change with them.

A vehicle can move from an ordinary road into an area where a lower speed is appropriate within seconds.

01

Static rules meet dynamic roads

Drivers encounter schools, hospitals, institutions and other sensitive areas during normal journeys.

02

Warnings alone have limits

A sign or alert can inform a driver, but safe behaviour still depends on timely human response.

03

Safety needs context

The same vehicle may need different speed behaviour depending on where it is and what is around it.

02 — THE SOLUTION

SAGE turns location into a safety signal.

SAGE combines digital geographic zones with vehicle-side decision logic to create a location-aware speed safety layer.

01

Know the location

Determine where the vehicle is in relation to mapped safety zones.

03 — WHY SAGE

A safety layer built around geography.

01

Location-aware

Safety behaviour can be tied to defined geographic boundaries instead of relying only on a fixed vehicle setting.

02

Zone-specific

Different areas can carry different safety rules, supporting more granular control than one universal limit.

03

Designed for offline operation

The SAGE concept supports locally available map and zone data, reducing dependence on continuous connectivity.

04

Updateable safety data

Geographic safety information can evolve as roads, institutions and designated zones change.

05

Integration-ready

Designed as a safety layer that can be integrated with mobility and vehicle-control systems.

06

Built for scale

The same underlying approach can support individual vehicles, fleets and managed mobility environments.

THE IDEA

Don't wait for unsafe speed to become an accident.

SAGE is being developed around a preventive approach: recognise higher-risk locations early and make speed behaviour responsive to the environment.
